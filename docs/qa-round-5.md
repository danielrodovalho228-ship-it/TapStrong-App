Fix: tag these with knee; treat any standing single-leg stance (`ankle.balance`) and load resting on the thighs as knee too; widen the audit to every standing move (13 more found: standing hinge holds, shrugs, standing stretches, Copenhagen — decide each). Kneeling hip flexor stretch must not reach 60+ with knee issues or 80-year-olds by default.
- **R5-02 Rest screen contradicts the "+1 rep" rule.** Player says "same weight", then the rest screen says "Try +5 lb" for Tom (60+ heart), bodyweight Wall push-up and Band chest press (Sam 80). - **R5-03 Stale workouts reused on later days.** A workout opened (or started and not finished) is reused days later with its old notes ("Chest rest today"), breaking the 72 h rule and variety; a Friday workout finished on Monday. `(tabs)/home.tsx:71-72` picks any `active`/`planned` workout with no date check; `workout/safety.ts:92-105` key has no date.
