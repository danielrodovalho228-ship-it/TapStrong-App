## Daniel's decisions (round 9)

1. **Joint care = 12 applies only to exercises that load the painful joint.** Knee pain: moves that load the knee total at most 12 working sets per rolling 7 days; a glute move with no knee load (e.g. clamshell) follows the age cap (adults 20). Tests: knee-pain adult never over 12 on knee-loading moves, glutes may reach 20 through non-knee moves.
2. **Repair and the finisher block count toward the weekly cap, but a Repair session is never cut by the cap.** Their working sets enter the 7-day history (also for recovery). Regular workouts, extra sets and "+1" respect the cap including those sets. A Repair session keeps its prescribed moves even if that muscle is at the cap.
3. **Kids 9–12 (mode off at launch): cap 10 sets** per primary muscle per 7 days (`WEEKLY_SETS.child = 10`). Test with the kids flag on.
4. **"+1 exercise?" can be offered again**, one exercise per tap, while the estimate is still under ~85% of the chosen time (chosen time > 45 min, never in deload), a safe exercise exists and the weekly cap allows it. Each accept keeps all previous exercises (R9-02).
